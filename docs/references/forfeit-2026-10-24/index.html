A forfeit for every kind of habit.

Setup
  1. Set the rule. Say what you'll do, by when, and how much you lose if you miss it. e.g. up by 6:30am, $15 on the line.
  2. Pick how to prove it. A Photo Forfeit works well: snap proof you're up, like a shot from your front step.
  3. Send evidence before the deadline. AI or a human reviewer checks the photo is fresh and real.
  4. Miss it, lose the money. No proof in the window and Forfeit charges your card automatically.
How verification works

You open the Forfeit camera, step outside, and snap once. The photo has to be live (not from your camera roll) and land inside the window. AI reviews it, and edge cases go to a human reviewer.
